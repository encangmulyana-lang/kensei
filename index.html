Why Kensei?

  • Scarcity you can verify: max supply 1,111 is enforced by the contract itself, not a promise. Contract: 0x1a92…6902
  • Premium, distinct art: each tier has its own artwork — Ronin, Rare Duelist, Epic Shogun, and a truly distinct Legendary master.
  • Cheap to join: first 555 at only 0.0002 BNB, the rest at 0.001 BNB. Max 10 per wallet, so no single wallet can sweep the collection.

  1. Connect your wallet on BNB Smart Chain in the Mint section below.
  2. Choose quantity (1–10) — the total updates automatically from the on-chain price.
  3. Click Mint and approve in your wallet. Gas is separate. Your NFT appears in your wallet after confirmation.
